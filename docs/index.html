A recorder and an editor, in one window

      
No round trip to a second app. Stop the recording and you are already in the timeline.

    


    

      

        01
        
Record what you choose

        
A window, a display, or one Chrome tab — from the app or from the web studio. Either way it lands back in the editor automatically.

      

      

        02
        
Clicks become zooms

        
Click activity is grouped and turned into eased zooms automatically. Adjust the timing, or let the camera follow the cursor.

      

      

        03
        
Your background

        
A colour, a gradient at any angle, your own image, or the recording itself blurred behind the frame.

Built for demos that have to be legible

      
The details that make a screen recording readable, handled by default.

    


    

      

        
A real macOS frame that holds still

        
An editable light or dark window frame with working traffic lights, an optional title and adjustable title-bar height. It stays fixed while the content zooms underneath it.

        
    
          
  • Non-destructive crop to a tab or a single pane
    • 
          
  • Canvas fitting preserves the content instead of clipping it
    • 
          
  • Source, landscape, portrait, square and 4:3 canvases
    • 
        

      

      

        
      

    


    

      

        
A cursor you can actually follow

        
Bounded smooth interpolation keeps the pointer readable at any zoom, with outlined styles, a gentle movement tilt, and click effects that still read on a small screen.

        
    
          
  • Ripple, pulse and press click effects
    • 
          
  • Keyboard shortcut badges — ordinary typed text is never collected
    • 
          
  • Four accent colours, adjustable size and shadow
    • 
        

      

      

        
      

    


    

      

        
One recording, every aspect ratio

        
Pick 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:3 and the composition re-fits itself — padding tightens as the aspects diverge, and the camera moves into the space that opens up instead of sitting on your content.

        
    
          
  • Background: colour, gradient, image, or the blurred recording
    • 
          
  • MP4 or MOV, source to 4K, 30 or 60 fps
    • 
          
  • The preview you scrub is the frame that exports
    • 
        

      

      

        
      

    


    

      

        
A timeline that does the obvious things

        
Scrub, move and resize segments, split at the playhead, duplicate, delete, undo and redo. Trim markers and playback speed stay non-destructive.

        
    
          
  • A separate camera layer you drag directly on the preview
    • 
          
  • Timed spotlights and text callouts with preview positioning
    • 
          
  • Automatic local saving, plus explicit project files
    • 
        

      

      

        
      

    

  






  

    

      
It never leaves your Mac

      

        No account, no telemetry, no upload step. Recordings are written
        straight to your disk, and the Chrome companion runs on your own
        machine.
      


      

        

          
Written locally

          
Recordings and projects land in ~/Movies/FocusFlow, deletable through the Trash.

        

        

          
A private loopback

          
Tab capture binds a random port on 127.0.0.1 with a per-session token and same-origin checks.

        

        

          
Permissions on demand

          
Camera and microphone are requested only when you switch them on, never at launch.

        

        

          
Readable source

          
MIT licensed, built with SwiftUI and ScreenCaptureKit. Read it, or build it yourself.

        

      

    

  






  

    

      
Installing takes one paste

      
Press the button, then paste into Terminal. Nothing to download by hand.

    


    

      

        01
        

          
Copy, then paste into Terminal

          
The command fetches the disk image from GitHub Releases, copies FocusFlow into Applications, and clears the download quarantine flag — with a live progress bar while it runs.

        

      

      

        02
        

          
Why the quarantine step is there

          
FocusFlow is signed ad‑hoc rather than with a paid Developer ID, so Gatekeeper would otherwise refuse the first launch. If you take the .dmg from the button instead, right‑click the app and choose Open the first time.

        

      

      

        03
        

          
Updates install themselves

          
From 0.4.0 onward FocusFlow checks GitHub Releases at launch. When there is a newer build a bar appears under the toolbar — press Install and relaunch and it downloads, swaps itself out and reopens. There is also FocusFlow → Check for Updates…. Nothing installs without you pressing the button.

        

      

      

        04
        

          
Allow Screen Recording

          
macOS asks on your first recording. FocusFlow then appears under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording.

        

      

      

        05
        

          
Or build it from source

          
Clone the repo and run ./build-app.sh. Requires macOS 14+ and the Apple Command Line Tools; ./check.sh runs the tests.
